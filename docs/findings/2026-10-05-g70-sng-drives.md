## Learned parameters: torqued valid, then reset by a MOCK route (observation + confirmed source fact)

- **torqued went valid** on route `12` (2026-10-04 ~18:35 EDT). Calibration rose from 70% to 98% during route `10` alone (716 s engaged above 34 mph), much faster than session 4b's estimate of ~1.3 h. The limiting bucket fills at a rate that depends on the road. While valid (routes `12`–`16`):
  - live latAccelFactor 3.80 → 3.69 (raw estimate 3.55–3.63);
  - friction 0.078 → 0.081.
  - So the engaged parts of routes `12` and `15` ran with a different effective tune than 4.0 / 0.07.
- **Route `17` fingerprinted as `MOCK`** (18 s, `fingerprintSource: can`). ~~Hypothesis: the device started before the car's CAN was up.~~ **Corrected (same session):** sunnypilot was disabled and re-enabled within ~2 s, and the 1 s offroad flip interrupted the firmware query. See [2026-10-05-g70-device-errors.md](2026-10-05-g70-device-errors.md) §2.
- **On the next route (`18`), torqued, paramsd and lagd all started from defaults:**

| | End of route `16` | Start of route `18` | End of route `1d` |
|---|---|---|---|
| torqued | valid, latAccelFactor 3.69, 7,723 points | not valid, 0 points | not valid, 59% calibrated, raw estimate 4.12 |
| paramsd steer ratio / angle offset | 14.26 / −2.55° | 14.1 / 0° | 14.28 / −2.28° |
| lagd delay | 0.184 s | 0.30 s (default) | 0.183 s |

- **Source (`82f58a9a8c`):**
  - `card.py` L162–165 copies the previous route's `CarParamsPersistent` to `CarParamsPrevRoute` at each start.
  - `torqued.py` L105–142 restores its cache only if (fingerprint, lateral tuning type, friction, latAccelFactor, version) match that previous route.
  - `paramsd.py` L203–215 and `lagd.py` L365–373 likewise drop their cache on a fingerprint mismatch.
  - **One `MOCK` start therefore discards all three learned states on the next real drive.**
- **Relearning on route `1a`** (engaged from 17 s):
  - the instant angle offset is back to −2.8° by 60 s;
  - the averaged offset takes 260–320 s;
  - lagd stays at 0.30 s until 336 s;
  - torqued needs more driving: 59% at the end of route `1d`, after ~60 min engaged (routes `18`–`1d`).
- **Consequences for this analysis:**
  - the SNG and LS episodes (route `1d`) and the closed-loop check routes (`10`, `1a`–`1d`) ran with the static tune 4.0 / 0.07, the same as sessions 4–7;
  - routes `12` and `15` are used only in the plant fit, which doesn't depend on the controller tune.

## Conclusions

1. **Coverage:** the new drives cover highway (D4) well. They give the first engaged stop-and-go data (2 episodes, 98 s), some manual stop-and-go (204 s), and very little steady 20–35 mph (126 s, one run ≥ 30 s).
2. **Stop-and-go weave:** **not demonstrated and not ruled out.**
   - D1 and D5 are "insufficient data".
   - In the two engaged episodes the wheel's weave-band motion (0.48°) is close to manual (0.42°), and the car follows the plan's wiggles only weakly (gain 0.29).
   - Nothing here supports a software change.
3. **Steady 20–35 mph:** with the earlier routes pooled, **D1 no and D5 no**. The engaged wheel moves less than the human's in the weave band.
4. **New finding: learned-state reset.** A single `MOCK` start (route `17`) discarded torqued, paramsd and lagd learning on the next drive (confirmed in source). torqued had just gone valid. This matters for any future comparison, and for the "check drive once torqued goes valid" step, which now has to wait again.
5. **The session 6 plant model holds at 35+ mph on new held-out drives.** At 20–35 mph it still under-predicts above 0.4 Hz, on very little data.

## What would answer the open stop-and-go question

Data, as in the plan, with the gaps made explicit:
- ≥ 3 engaged and ≥ 3 manual SNG episodes of ≥ 20 s, ideally in the **same kind of scene** (the same road type, or the same stretch);
- steady 20–35 mph engaged runs ≥ 30 s on straight, well-marked roads;
- a note or bookmark of when a weave is felt. Bookmarks were not checked in these logs.

No device change or software change is proposed.
