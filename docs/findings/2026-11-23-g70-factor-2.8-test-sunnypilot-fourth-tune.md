## 3. Reading (inferences)

- The pattern of the evening tests holds on the new limits: a higher factor calms the wheel and loosens the lane holding. Matching the best test's steering strength did not reproduce its result; that test also had a different limit, rate limits and road.
- At the 270 limit the looser lane holding cost more than the calmer wheel gained. With standard values, a factor near 2.5 looks better than 2.8.
- The shared-road rows are the first direct comparison of sunnypilot's tune with ours on the same road: about twice the lane movement, on a short overlap.
