## Results

### 1. There is no 0.1 Hz oscillation (correction)

The earlier "dominant ~0.1 Hz" came from the lower edge of the search band in `lateral_metrics.py` (0.1–2 Hz).

With 60 s windows (0.017 Hz resolution), the lane-offset power falls steadily from the lowest bin, with no peak:

| Band (Hz) | Route 6 | Route 7 | Route 8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.017–0.05 | 0.39 | 0.43 | 0.29 |
| 0.05–0.1 | 0.42 | 0.36 | 0.36 |
| 0.1–0.2 | 0.15 | 0.15 | 0.24 |
| 0.2–0.5 | 0.03 | 0.05 | 0.10 |
| 0.5–2 | 0.01 | 0.01 | 0.01 |

The values are shares of the offset power (detrended with a 120 s moving average).

So the "drift" is a slow lane-position variation on a 10–60 s scale, not a periodic oscillation.

### 2. Most of it follows road curvature: the car moves toward the inside of curves

| | Route 6 | Route 7 | Route 8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offset variance explained by road curvature (linear fit) | **82%** | **80%** | **64%** |
| Fitted shift toward the inside, per 1/1000 m curvature (1 km radius) | 0.39 m | 0.36 m | 0.24 m |
| Mean device position, left curves (radius 500–2000 m) | −0.44 m | −0.49 m | −0.30 m |
| Mean device position, near-straight (radius > 2 km) | −0.19 m | −0.19 m | −0.15 m |
| Mean device position, right curves (radius 500–2000 m) | +0.23 m | +0.11 m | +0.13 m |

- Road curvature comes from the lane centre at 15–60 m ahead.
- Positions are relative to the lane centre; negative = left.
- The car shifts toward the **inside** of curves in both directions, on top of a **static ~0.15–0.19 m left bias** of the device.
- What remains after the curvature fit is small (18–36% of the variance) and broadband.
- Route 8 had a smaller shift than routes 6/7. Its roads may differ, so this can't be attributed to the tune.

### 3. The motion is real, not perception noise

- The lane lines move together:
  - lane-width variation std (as half-width): 0.024–0.028 m;
  - offset variation std: 0.10–0.13 m.
- The rate of change of q0 is consistent with v × (device heading relative to the lane):
  - coherence 0.79 at 0.05–0.25 Hz;
  - gain 0.81–0.83;
  - phase within ±3.5°.
  - The gain below 1 is unexplained. The heading comes from the line slope over 0–10 m, which is approximate.

### 4. The model plans it and the car executes it

- **Plan:** the model's path returns toward its preferred position. It keeps 83–88% of a deviation at 1 s, 54–62% at 2 s and 28–50% at 3 s. The "preferred position" itself includes the curve-inside shift above.
- **Execution (0.05–0.25 Hz):**
  - controlsd's desired curvature equals the model's (gain 1.00, coherence 1.00).
  - Actual curvature from the Kalman yaw rate vs desired: gain 1.08 / 1.08 / **1.02** (route 8, new tune), phase within ±2.5°, coherence ≥ 0.98.
- **Plan vs outcome over 1 s:**
  - realised change vs planned change: slope 0.79–0.86, correlation 0.78–0.82;
  - realised − planned position: std 0.04–0.05 m, mean within 0.02 m.

## Interpretation (hypothesis)

- The remaining slow lane-position variation is **the built-in model's own path choice**:
  - it positions the car toward the inside of curves (curve-cutting, ~0.25–0.4 m in a 1 km curve);
  - it holds a static ~0.15–0.19 m left bias.
- The controller follows the plan closely in this band.
- So neither the torque tune nor other controller changes would remove it.
- **Possibly related:** some of the "oversteer in curves" the user still feels may be this inside-of-curve positioning, not torque overshoot (actual/desired is now 1.007). That needs the user's description to confirm.
- Margin estimate: in left curves the device sits up to ~0.5 m left of centre in lanes 3.0–3.3 m wide. If the device is mounted on the car's centreline and the car is ~1.85 m wide (assumed, not measured), that leaves roughly 0.1–0.2 m between the left tyres and the line. This is approximate.

## Limitations

- **No human reference.** There is no manual driving on these roads, so it's unknown whether this curve-cutting is more than a human would do on the same roads.
- q0 is the **device** position. Its lateral mount offset from the car's centreline is unknown, so the static bias can't be split into mount offset vs model bias.
- Lane lines and road curvature are the model's own perception. Only one model has been observed.
- Before/after (routes 6/7 vs 8) are different drives, roads not controlled.
- The detrend windows (20 s, 120 s) and thresholds are analysis choices. The conclusions hold across both windows, but the exact percentages depend on them.

### Low-speed data drive protocol (agreed with the user, 2026-10-04)

**Road priority:**

1. **Curvy surface road at 30–35 mph**, with clear markings and few stops. This is the main target: sustained low speed, varied curvature, mostly visible lane lines. Include some time at 25–30 mph.
2. **Stop-and-go traffic**, whenever it happens. It's the actual complaint but can't be planned.
3. **Curvy state highway at 45–55 mph.** No data yet between 20 and 25 m/s, so this shows whether behaviour changes gradually with speed (the controller gain schedule runs from 2.0 at 15 m/s to 1.0 at 30 m/s). It also fills `torqued`'s slow buckets faster.
4. **Interstate:** lowest value. There's already about an hour of it.

**On each stretch:**

- Drive it **engaged and then manually**: same stretch and direction, similar traffic, at least 3–5 min each if possible.
- **Engaged:** hands off the wheel (any steering override contaminates the data); blinkers only when needed.
- **Manual:** make sure lateral is really off. With MADS on, steering can stay active without cruise, so turn lateral off as well. Drive centred, the way you normally would.
- Note the approximate clock times or landmarks of each stretch.
- Videos will be pulled with the rlogs (standing authorization, 2026-10-04).

## Options (each a separate bounded change; user decision)

1. **Human reference, no software change:** a few minutes of manual driving on a curvy stretch already driven engaged (e.g. route 8's road). It gives the human curve shift and bias from the same perception. This is the cheapest way to tell whether the model is unusual.
2. **Measure the device mount's lateral offset** from the car's centreline. This turns the device bias into a car bias.
3. **Model choice:** selecting a downloaded model bundle moves the device to `modeld_v2`. That also activates `CameraOffset`/`PlanplusControl`, so it changes several things at once. It needs its own proposal: which model, download, device-load check, before/after on the same roads, rollback.
4. **Not indicated:** further controller/torque changes for this symptom, since tracking in this band is already close to 1.
