  - **A-same-day** = `0x26` (dark);

  - **`0x27`** descriptively (daylight, road unknown).
- **Speed:** H = v ≥ 24.6 m/s (55 mph). Below 55 mph: descriptive only (§6).
- **Selections** (the same functions as offline):
  - straight-clean = `s11_holdjump.select`;
  - all-engaged = `s11_eval.select_all`.
  - Jitter metrics on runs ≥ 12 s (`hj.runs(sel & H & road, 1200, 0)`, the s11_eval statistics rule). Comfort and weave metrics on runs ≥ 8 s with 2 s trimmed (Amendment 5).
- **Pooling:** per-run values weighted by run length (RMS pooled as the weighted mean square; rates as total counts over total minutes).

### 3.2 Metrics and criteria (B vs A-primary; % = (B − A)/A)

The signals are logged, with no simulation. The angle is `carState.steeringAngleDeg` (0.1° quantized). The request is `desiredCurvature · v²`, using `controlsState.desiredCurvature`, which is the unfiltered request in both arms (proposal §1 log semantics). The measurement is V0's map `A·(ang − aoff) + B` (the `s11_dataset` reconstruction of `actualLateralAccel`).

1. **Jitter** (straight-clean H): `s11_eval.stats` on the logged angle per run.
   - **Pass if** jumps/min ≥ 30% lower **and** angle 0.7–4 Hz RMS (T) ≥ 20% lower.
   - Hold fraction and reversals/min are reported too.
2. **Weave** (straight-clean H): angle 0.2–0.7 Hz RMS (W, `stats`), and lane-centre offset 0.05–0.7 Hz RMS (`s12_comfort.lane_offset_runs`; modelV2 lane lines, both probabilities > 0.5).
   - **Pass if** neither is up by > 15%.
3. **Comfort:**
   - **(a)** steering-proxy lateral jerk RMS (`s11_eval.comfort`, straight-clean H): **pass if not up** (B ≤ A).
   - **(b)** IMU `a_y` 0.5–4 Hz RMS (`s12_comfort.a_y_on_grid` from `s12_imu_extract.py` output; straight-clean H): **pass if not up by > 10%**.
     - **Gate:** |corr| ≥ 0.8 of the 0.05–0.5 Hz bands of `a_y` and v × gyro yaw rate (`s12_gate_diag.gyro_z_on_grid`), on all-engaged H samples, per arm.
     - The gate is redefined against v × yaw rate, as validation findings §2.1.1 required. An arm that fails it has invalid IMU numbers, and (b) is then reported as not evaluable.
   - **(c)** 20 Hz line in `steeringTorqueEps` and column `steeringTorque` 8–20 Hz RMS (`s12_comfort.line_amp`, Amendment 5 definitions; all-engaged H): **pass if neither is up by > 20%**.
4. **Tracking** (all-engaged H):
   - **(a)** mean |request − measurement|: **pass if not up by > 30%**.
   - **(b)** curve entry/exit lag: events by `s12_curves.events` on the 0.5 s mean |request|, inside all-engaged H runs ≥ 12 s on B's road. Lag by `s12_curves.lag_s` between the logged request and the logged measurement in the −2…+6 s window. **Pass if the mean lag is not up by > 0.15 s.**
   - **(c)** mean |lane-centre offset| in curves: samples with 0.5 s-mean |request| ≥ 0.5 m/s², both lane probabilities > 0.5. **Pass if not up by > 0.05 m.**
   - **(d)** steering-override and hands-on events: rising edges of `steeringPressed` while active, per engaged hour, plus disengagements. **Reported with context.** "Attributable to B" cannot be judged without video, so this criterion is **pending** until the videos are pulled.
5. **Driver's assessment:** the user's own words about the B drive, recorded verbatim, collected before the numbers are shown.

**Uncertainty:** each metric gets a 60 s block bootstrap CI (2,000 resamples, seed 0) for B and A-primary. The per-route A spread is reported next to it.

**Verdict wording:** "passes or fails the pre-registered §6 criterion on this data". With B below the §6 minimum (§2.3), every verdict is labelled **preliminary**.
