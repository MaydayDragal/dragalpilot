## 1. The change

**Commits on `dragal/custom`** (local, not pushed), on top of the installed `c027a92857`: `268040c361` + `8593b66148` + `66b22ef83f` + `89bbe60ab1`. **Final head `89bbe60ab127cf71d238856a19c1e74b16bc36bd`.**

**Validated configuration:** device params `NeuralNetworkLateralControl`, `EnforceTorqueControl`, `LateralJerkTorqueController` and `TorqueParamsOverrideEnabled` all off (V0 with the extension inactive).
- Source fact: with NNLC or `EnforceTorqueControl` on, sunnypilot re-runs `configure_torque_tune` without the deadzone (`openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/car/interfaces.py` L67–69). D2 would then silently drop to 0 while C4 stays.
- `install.sh` and `precheck_reboot.sh` refuse to proceed unless all four params are off.

**Log semantics after install** (for later analysis):
- `controlsState.desiredCurvature`, `actuators.curvature` and `curvature_limited` stay **unfiltered**.
- `lateralControlState.torqueState.desiredLateralAccel` and `desiredLateralJerk` are **filtered**.
- The filter state itself is not logged. Replays use the logged desiredCurvature as the pre-filter input.

**What it doesn't touch:**
- panda safety and firmware (no reflash);
- native code, params keys and cereal schema;
- longitudinal control.

**CarParams:**
- `lateralTuning.torque.steeringAngleDeadzoneDeg` changes from 0 to 0.4 on this car.
- Source fact: the torqued, paramsd and lagd restore keys don't include it (validation findings §2.2), so all learned state is kept.

**Effect on the car (offline model evidence only; not validated on-car):**
- **Jitter, on straight highway:** about half the hold-and-jump steps (−45…−60%), fast wheel motion −33…−42%, reversals −56…−60%.
- **Weave:** the 0.2–0.7 Hz band is 7–25% lower.
- **Comfort proxy:** steering-derived lateral jerk −23…−31%, step events −29…−59%.
- **Costs** (pre-registered red flags, accepted by the user as a known trade-off, 2026-10-07):
  - mean tracking error about +20% (≈ +0.01 m/s²);
  - in curve entries and exits, about +0.09 s more lag and +28% mean error;
  - +17–19° extra phase lag at 0.6 Hz;
  - slower settling of small steps at 75 mph (a ~0.03 m/s² residual).
- **Not modelled:** speeds below 55 mph (no validated model) and engaged stop-and-go (never active at a standstill in the logs). Below 55 mph the deadzone shrinks with v² in lateral-accel terms. The 2 Hz filter applies at every speed.

**How it's deployed:** a git bundle of the commits, verified by sha256 on the device, then fetch and fast-forward. That is the same mechanism as the three earlier deployments.

## 6. On-car A/B protocol (draft; fixed before the first B drive)

- **A** = `c027a92857` (current), **B** = D2C4.
- **Drives:**
  - the same road and direction for A and B, in comparable traffic and weather;
  - engaged, hands off where safe and legal;
  - at least 10 min of straight highway at ≥ 55 mph and at least 10 curve entries/exits per arm;
  - A drives from the existing E4 data on the same road may be used if they exist.
  - Phone wheel video and device bookmarks as in session 10.
  - The driver can disengage at any time; any unexpected behaviour ends B and triggers the rollback discussion.
- **Analysis:** the same tools and definitions as offline (validation findings §2.1, Amendment 5 metrics, `s12_comfort.py`, `s11_eval.stats`), on rlogs and both road videos.
- **Success criteria for B vs A** (straight-clean ≥ 55 mph unless stated):
  1. **Jitter:** jumps/min ≥ 30% lower **and** angle 0.7–4 Hz RMS ≥ 20% lower. These are deliberately looser than the model's −45…−60% / −33…−42%, because roads and days differ.
  2. **Weave:** angle 0.2–0.7 Hz RMS and lane-centre-offset 0.05–0.7 Hz RMS not up > 15%.
  3. **Comfort:**
     - steering-proxy lateral jerk RMS not up;
     - IMU lateral-accel 0.5–4 Hz RMS not up > 10%, with the gate redefined against v × gyro yaw rate (validation findings §2.1.1);
     - 20 Hz EPS line and column 8–20 Hz not up > 20%.
  4. **Tracking, all engaged ≥ 55 mph:**
     - mean |request − measurement| not up > 30%;
     - curve entry/exit lag not up > 0.15 s;
     - mean |lane-centre offset| in curves not up > 0.05 m;
     - no new steering-override or hands-on events attributable to B.
  5. **Driver's assessment:** the twitch is noticeably reduced, with no new discomfort in curves.
- **Below 55 mph** (not modelled): reported descriptively. Any regression the driver notices there is a failure.
