What changes on the car:
- **Steering (tune):** feedforward torque per unit of desired lateral acceleration drops to 0.675× (2.7/4.0); the friction term drops from ±0.11 to ±0.07 of max torque. `torqued` restarts learning from the new values (its cache key includes the offline values) and may move ±30% (2.8–5.2). `paramsd` keeps its learned steerRatio (~14.1 is within 0.5–2× of the new value), so the steerRatio change has little practical effect.
- **0x484:** the panda stops forwarding the stock camera's HDA11_MFC (0x484) to the car, which upstream used to suppress the car's "grasp not detected" hands-on warning. 0x484 is added to the TX allowlist, but openpilot never sends it on this platform; **no actuation limit is changed.** With `check_relay`, a 0x484 frame seen on the car side while in the Hyundai mode triggers relay-malfunction protection, which blocks openpilot's TX and is fail-safe.
- **Scope:** the safety change applies to every non-long camera-SCC Hyundai config (also `HYUNDAI_KONA_2022`, `HYUNDAI_KONA_EV_2022` in source), as upstream intended. Longitudinal camera-SCC and radar-SCC configs are unchanged; the tests assert this. Only this car runs this build.

## 4. Experiment definition (next drive)

- **Question 1 (tune):** does the ping-pong / curve overshoot reduce without understeer or faults?
  - Compare against routes 6/7 (same software otherwise), ideally on the same roads at similar speeds.
  - Success: in sustained curves, actual/desired lat accel within 0.95–1.04 (baseline 1.07); integrator opposing feedforward in clearly fewer curves (baseline 71–100%); share of control-error power in 0.5–3 Hz above 25 m/s lower than baseline (0.54–0.70); mean |error| not worse than +20% (baseline ~0.064); 0 steer faults; saturation ≤ 1%. Plus the driver's subjective report.
  - Failure: understeer (ratio < 0.93, drifting wide in curves), more oscillation, any steering fault or new alert.
- **Question 2 (0x484):** does the cluster hands-on/"grasp" warning stop during engaged driving?
  - Evidence: the user's observation (times), the camera's `LFA_SysWarning` episodes on bus 2 (still logged), the pandad flash/signature log line, and no relay-malfunction/controls-blocked events.
  - Failure: relay malfunction, any new cluster fault or HDA/LFA error, or the warning persists.

## 6. Rollback

- `cd /data/openpilot && git checkout 7e9d0755d9` (or `dragal/custom~7`), then a reboot (explicit authorization). `pandad` reflashes the shipped firmware (`bf8e3dab…`) because the signature differs.
- Further back: `git checkout dev` → baseline `0b2c431d` (same shipped firmware).
- If a flash ever leaves the panda in the bootstub, `pandad` runs its internal-panda recovery (`HARDWARE.recover_internal_panda()`, `panda.recover()`). Without a working panda, openpilot cannot control the car; the harness's stock behaviour is the fallback (not tested on this car).
- `torqued` will have restarted learning; rolling back resets it again (cache key changes back).
