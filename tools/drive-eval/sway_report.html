Why: the steering loop by frequency

Lane offset feeds the plan, the plan feeds the request, the car follows the request, and that moves the car in the lane. Ping-pong lives at 0.2–0.45 Hz (one swing every 1–2.5 s), wobble above 0.7 Hz. Estimated from normal driving, so read the shapes more than single values.

How to read this

A swing is one move of the lane-centre offset from one turning point to the next (reversals under 3 cm ignored). Its period is twice its duration. Ping-pong = period 2–5 s; wander = slower than 5 s; a train = four or more ping-pong swings in a row. Wobble = swings of lateral acceleration faster than 1.6 s and at least 0.15 m/s². Follow delay = how late the car's lateral acceleration is behind the request. Amplification above 1 means the loop makes a lane disturbance at that frequency bigger.
